Biodata

I am tri — fevnem on GitHub. I write software in small pieces and try to keep each piece whole: something you can open, understand and run to the end. Most of it starts as a question I cannot leave alone — how a satellite can tell you where you stand, how thirty-two cases can be ordered so a reader wants the next one, why a page that will not keep still is a page nobody finishes.

The tools change with the problem; the shape of the work does not. Go when a thing has to stay up and stay small. The browser when the idea is the interface. Plain text, plain arithmetic, and as few moving parts as I can get away with. I am more interested in the seam where a system meets a person than in the system on its own.

What follows is the chronology, then the work grouped by what it is.
